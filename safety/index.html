Sirkl - Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Sirkl - Private Groups is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Our Standards:

  • Sirkl prohibits any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors
  • All groups are private and invite-only — no public discovery or open access
  • Users can report inappropriate content directly within the app
  • Reported content is reviewed and actioned promptly
  • Accounts found sharing CSAE content are permanently banned and reported to relevant authorities

Contact:
To report child safety concerns, contact us at: hello@sirkl.app
