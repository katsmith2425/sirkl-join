PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What We Collect

  • Name and profile photo you provide
  • Email address (optional, only if you choose to add it)
  • Apple ID (used for authentication via Sign in with Apple)
  • Messages, events, and polls you create within the app
  • Device push notification token (for notifications)

How We Use It

  • To provide the Sirkl app experience
  • To send push notifications about group activity
  • To display your profile to members of shared Sirkls

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not share your information with third parties except as required to operate the service (Supabase for database/storage, Expo for push notifications, RevenueCat for payments)

ZIP code (Event Hub)
If you use the Event Hub, we ask for your ZIP code so we can show public events in your area. We store it with your account. You can change it anytime in the Event Hub. We don't use it to track your location.

Event Hub activity
When you open an Event Hub event, we record that you viewed it. Businesses that post events see only totals, such as how many members viewed their event, how many groups added it, and how many people RSVP'd. They never see your name or who you are. If you report an event or hide a host, we store that so we can review reports and keep hidden hosts out of your feed.

Private messages
If you send or receive private messages, we store the messages, who sent them, who received them, and when, so they can be delivered and shown to you. Private messages can only be exchanged between members who share a group. If you report a message, we save a copy of it so our moderation team can review it. You can turn off private messages in Edit Profile and block other members at any time.

Data Storage
Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers located in the United States.

Account Deletion
To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the support link below.
